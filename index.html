About

hidden=until-found

Experimental

What it does. Reveals hidden content when the user uses the browser's in-page search (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) and a match is found.

Why it is interesting. It lets long pages keep progressive disclosure without breaking find-in-page.

Why not a hard dependency. Support is still limited; content must remain accessible via ordinary disclosure when unsupported.

View transitions

Experimental

What it does. Smoothly animates between two DOM states using the browser's native cross-document or same-document view transition API.

Why it is interesting. It gives SPA-like transitions without the routing complexity or performance cost of a full framework.
